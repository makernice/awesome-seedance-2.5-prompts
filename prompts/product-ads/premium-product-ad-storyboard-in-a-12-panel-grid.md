Create a premium commercial storyboard presentation for a product advertisement.

Style:
Professional advertising agency storyboard, clean white background with navy blue accents, modern infographic layout, photorealistic product photography, premium minimal design.

A3 landscape. 12 storyboard panels in a 4×3 grid. Each panel includes:
• Shot number
• Time

Title: HAN RIVER WET & DRY 2-IN-1 STEAM IRON
Subtitle: ASMR UNBOXING (POV HAND)

• Style: POV Hand, ASMR, Premium Product Commercial
• Audience: Home Users, Housewives, Young Adults
• Voiceover: None (Pure ASMR)
• Audio: Tapping, Cardboard, Crinkle, Click, Steam, Water Pouring

Storyboard:

PART 1 (0–10s)
1. Box on wooden table. "NEW!"
2. Finger traces logo. "LET'S OPEN!"
3. Cut tape with utility knife. "SLICE..."
4. Open box revealing steam iron, manual, accessories. "WOW!"

PART 2 (10–20s)
5. Hold instruction manual. "MANUAL"

9. Rotate handle 180°. "180°"
10. Pour water into tank. "FILL"
11. Press steam button with visible steam. "PSSSH~"

✓ 150 ml Tank

Home, Travel, Small Apartment

Lightweight, Easy to Use, Fast Heating, Strong Steam, Safe & Reliable

Warm wooden tabletop, soft natural light, shallow depth of field, POV hands only, no faces, luxury commercial product photography, consistent angle.

Typography:
Bold navy sans-serif headings, black body text, white handwritten doodles over each image.

Generate an agency-quality, print-ready storyboard with sharp, realistic details and balanced spacing.
