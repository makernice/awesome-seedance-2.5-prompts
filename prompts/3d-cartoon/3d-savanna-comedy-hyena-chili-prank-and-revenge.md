```text
Create a highly detailed 30-second cinematic 3D animated fantasy comedy film.

FORMAT:
30 seconds, 16:9 widescreen, high-quality 3D animation, cinematic lighting, detailed character textures, smooth animation and expressive facial movements.

CHARACTERS:

1. HYENA:
   A large grey-brown spotted hyena with dark spots, big pointed ears, expressive eyes, a black nose and a mischievous personality. Maintain the same character design throughout.

2. SKUNK:
   A tiny adorable black-and-white skunk with a white stripe running down its head and back.

3. HAMSTER:
   A small chubby cream-colored hamster with soft fluffy fur, tiny paws and expressive eyes.

4. WHITE RABBIT:
   A cute fluffy white rabbit with long upright ears and big expressive eyes.

Keep all four characters visually consistent throughout the video.

ENVIRONMENT:

A beautiful African savanna with golden dry grass, scattered acacia trees, a large shady tree, a small natural pond and distant green grasslands.

The daytime scenes have bright natural sunlight. The nighttime scenes have a deep blue sky and soft moonlight.

STORY — EXACTLY 30 SECONDS:

0–4 SECONDS — THE SNEAKY HYENA

Under a large acacia tree, the tiny skunk, chubby hamster and fluffy white rabbit are sleeping peacefully together.

A mischievous spotted hyena quietly approaches them from behind the tree.

He looks around with a sneaky smile and carefully picks up a bright red chili pepper.

He leans toward the sleeping hamster and secretly places the chili pepper near its mouth.

Use a close-up showing the hyena's mischievous expression and the sleeping animals.

4–8 SECONDS — THE SPICY SURPRISE

The hamster suddenly reacts to the extremely spicy chili.

Its entire body turns bright glowing red, its eyes become spinning spirals and its tongue sticks out.

The hamster looks completely dizzy and shocked.

The skunk and white rabbit wake up and stare at their glowing red friend with surprised expressions.

Show expressive close-ups of all three animals.

8–12 SECONDS — CHAOS IN THE SAVANNA

Cut to a wide shot of the three tiny animals huddled beneath the large tree.

Suddenly, a bright fiery explosion erupts in the nearby savanna, sending a dramatic flame and smoke into the air.

Cut to the nearby pond where a fiery object falls into the water, creating a large splash and ripples.

The spotted hyena loses his balance and tumbles backward onto the dry grass, rolling over dramatically.

12–16 SECONDS — THE HYENA FALLS

Show the hyena rolling on his back with his legs raised and mouth open.

He looks dizzy and helpless.

Cut to the pond where the skunk, hamster and rabbit are together in the shallow water.

They look toward one another with determined expressions.

Use a cinematic medium-wide shot with the beautiful savanna landscape behind them.

16–20 SECONDS — NIGHTTIME REVENGE

Transition naturally to nighttime beneath the same large acacia tree.

The spotted hyena is sleeping deeply on the ground.

The three little animals quietly approach him.

The skunk carries a thick rope, while the hamster and rabbit bring a basket full of bright red chili peppers.

They carefully prepare a playful revenge plan while the hyena remains asleep.

20–24 SECONDS — THE SECRET PLAN

Show the skunk carefully wrapping the rope around the sleeping hyena's body.

The hyena is now securely tied and begins to wake up.

The three little animals stand around him.

The skunk approaches his face holding a large red chili pepper.

The hamster and rabbit stand beside a basket overflowing with red chili peppers.

The hyena opens his eyes in confusion.

24–27 SECONDS — THE SPICY REVENGE

The hyena finds himself surrounded by the three little animals.

The skunk brings red chili peppers close to his mouth.

Show a funny close-up of the hyena's shocked face as red chili peppers appear in his mouth.

The hamster and rabbit watch with mischievous smiles.

Suddenly, a dramatic burst of fire erupts from behind a nearby bush, illuminating the nighttime savanna.

27–30 SECONDS — FINAL COMEDY SHOT

Show the three little animals standing together in the foreground.

The skunk, hamster and white rabbit look toward the hyena with satisfied expressions.

The tied hyena remains in the background, looking shocked and overwhelmed by the spicy revenge.

A tall column of fire glows behind the nearby bush.

End with a cinematic wide shot of all the characters under the beautiful dark-blue night sky.

VISUAL STYLE:

Premium cinematic 3D animated fantasy comedy.

Beautiful detailed character fur.

Expressive animated faces.

High-quality realistic fur simulation.

Natural animal body movements.

Detailed African savanna environment.

Beautiful acacia trees and golden grass.

Realistic water splashes and ripples.

Dynamic fire and smoke simulation.

Professional cinematic lighting.

Smooth character animation.

Rich natural colors.

Humorous facial expressions.

CAMERA:

Use cinematic wide shots, medium shots, character close-ups, reaction shots, low-angle shots and smooth tracking movements.

Use close-ups for the hamster's spicy reaction and the hyena's shocked expressions.

Maintain clear visibility of all important characters during group scenes.

CONTINUITY:

Keep the same four characters throughout the entire film.

Maintain consistent character proportions, colors, facial features and fur textures.

The daytime savanna and nighttime tree environment must remain visually consistent.

The chili peppers must remain bright red.

The rope must remain visible after tying the hyena.

Every action must connect naturally to the next scene.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:

No humans.
No additional animals.
No character duplication.
No character design changes.
No random scene changes.
No disappearing characters.
No distorted faces.
No extra limbs.
No broken anatomy.
No unrealistic movements.
No photorealistic live-action humans.
No subtitles.
No text.
No logos.
No watermark.

FINAL OUTPUT:

A funny, visually engaging 30-second cinematic 3D animated comedy about a mischievous spotted hyena, three adorable little animal friends and their hilarious spicy revenge.

Keep the storytelling clear, entertaining and visually consistent from beginning to end.
```
