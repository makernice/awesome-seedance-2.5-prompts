```text
A 26-second VERTICAL 9:16 gothic comedy in premium stylized 3D feature-animation style. Expressive character acting, detailed velvet, lace, hair and cosmetics, soft cinematic lighting, crisp close-ups and lively editing. Match the animation style of @image1. This is a stylized animated film, not live action. Natural-speed actions, no slow motion.

REFERENCES
@image1 is the character identity and starting costume reference:
LEFT character = the witch makeup artist.
RIGHT character = the ghost woman BEFORE her makeover.
Preserve their faces, body proportions, skin colors and starting clothes. The reference is not the first-frame composition. Both characters are adults.
No video reference is required. Follow the choreography and timeline below.

CHARACTERS
WITCH: shorter, curvy, confident adult woman with warm peach skin, amber eyes, sculpted dark purple waves with a silver forelock, burgundy lipstick, a tilted black witch hat with a plum band and gold crescent, gold crescent earrings, plum velvet midi dress, black belt and black heeled ankle boots. Dry humor, precise gestures, unimpressed expressions. Her appearance never changes.

GHOST: much taller, slender adult woman with pale gray-blue skin, large gray-green eyes, purple under-eye shadows, long tangled dark hair, an antique ivory dress with lace collar and cuffs, frayed hem and gray lace-up shoes. Initially awkward and anxious. Keep her facial identity and pale skin throughout the makeover. Only her makeup, hairstyle and later accessories change.

ENVIRONMENT AND BLOCKING
An intimate gothic dressing room with dark plum walls, ornate antique gold furniture, cobwebs in the upper corners, candles and warm bulbs around a large vanity mirror. Cool blue light enters through a rear doorway on screen right.
The vanity and mirror occupy the left and central background. Two separate plum upholstered seats stand in front of the vanity: the witch's seat on screen left and the client's seat on screen right.
Once the makeover starts, the witch stands on the client's screen-left side. Maintain these positions across cuts.
Cosmetics are arranged on the vanity: lipstick, concealer, orange sponge, eyeshadow brush, liner, false lashes, tweezers, lip pencil, lip brush, hairspray, hairpins, a crescent choker and a powder compact.
Keep backgrounds spatially consistent. Reflections show the same characters and current actions, never extra people.

TIMELINE

0.00-1.00 | THE FAILED SCARE
Medium two-shot, slightly angled toward the vanity. The witch is seated on screen left, already applying burgundy lipstick with her right hand.
The ghost steps into view behind her, raises both hands beside her face with fingers spread, leans forward and exclaims, "Boo!"
The witch does not flinch. Her lipstick hand stays steady.

1.00-2.70 | COMPLETELY UNIMPRESSED
Cut to a close-up of the witch. She slowly shifts only her eyes toward the ghost, maintaining a bored expression.
At approximately 1.10 she gives a short disapproving "Mm-mm."
She finishes one small lipstick stroke, lowers the lipstick and gives a second "Mm-mm." at approximately 2.10.
Make these two separate brief vocal responses. No frightened reaction.

2.70-4.00 | TAKE A SEAT
Return to the two-shot. The witch places the lipstick on the vanity and gestures toward the empty client seat with an open hand.
The ghost lowers her raised hands, steps around the chair and sits. Show her hips settling onto the cushion.
The witch rises beside her and reaches for the concealer. Cut only after the ghost is seated.

4.00-5.20 | CONCEALER
Frontal close-up of the ghost, looking uncertain.
The witch's right hand enters from screen left and places one small beige concealer dab beneath each eye. Both marks remain visible until blended.
The ghost follows the applicator with her eyes without moving her head excessively.

5.20-7.00 | SPONGE REACTION
A slightly tighter close-up. An orange sponge gently presses and releases against the ghost's cheek and under-eye area, visibly blending the concealer.
The ghost briefly scrunches her nose and bares her teeth in an awkward comic grimace, then relaxes.
The sponge deforms slightly on contact. It never passes through her face.

7.00-9.30 | EYESHADOW
Macro of a closed eyelid. A soft brush deposits shimmering smoky plum eyeshadow with one controlled sweep.
Cut to a matching close-up of the other eyelid receiving the same treatment.
By the end, both eyelids have matching finished eyeshadow. Preserve her natural eye shape.

9.30-11.50 | EYELINER
Close-up of one closed eye. The witch draws a clean dark line along the upper lash line with a small outward flick.
Use a brief matching insert to establish the other eye completed.
The tool touches the eyelid, never the eyeball.

11.50-13.50 | LASHES
Tight close-up. Tweezers place a false lash strip onto the upper lash line while that eye is closed.
The witch gives a quiet evaluative "Mm-mm." around 12.00.
A short cut reveals both lash strips properly attached. The ghost opens both eyes and blinks once. No loose floating eyelashes.

13.50-15.30 | LIP CONTOUR
Close-up of the mouth. A burgundy pencil traces the cupid's bow and continues along the lip edge.
A brief editing ellipse reveals the completed outline. Keep her lips the same anatomical size.

15.30-17.50 | LIP COLOR
A fine lip brush fills the outlined lips with deep berry-red color.
The witch lightly supports the underside of the ghost's chin with one fingertip, then withdraws her hand.
The ghost gives a small pleased smile. Her face remains recognizably the same.

17.50-20.70 | HAIR TRANSFORMATION
Medium shot. The witch gathers the ghost's long hair upward with both hands.
Use two quick action-matched cuts showing successive stages: gathered hair, then a secured tall sculpted updo. The witch visibly inserts hairpins.
Leave two fine face-framing strands. Add one small gold crescent and one gold star hair ornament.
The witch picks up hairspray from the vanity and releases one short visible mist above the hairstyle, directed away from the ghost's eyes. The ghost briefly widens her eyes.
Hair is physically styled, not magically replaced.

20.70-23.00 | FIRST LOOK
Cut to an over-the-shoulder mirror composition with both women's reflections clearly visible.
The ghost turns her head slightly to inspect the makeup and updo, then smiles in surprise.
The witch watches with a satisfied knowing smile.
The mirror accurately reflects the completed hair and makeup.

23.00-25.00 | THE CHOKER
Close-up of the neck below the existing lace collar. The witch places a narrow black choker around the exposed neck above the collar and fastens it at the back.
A small gold crescent pendant settles at the front.
The choker remains on for the rest of the film. Do not fasten it again in Part 2.

25.00-26.00 | PREPARE THE CONTINUATION CUT
Medium-close frontal view of the seated ghost, with the witch on screen left.
The witch holds an open round powder compact in her LEFT hand. With her RIGHT hand she lifts a fluffy powder brush toward the ghost's RIGHT cheek, the cheek visible on screen left.
The ghost closes her eyes and turns her face very slightly toward screen right.
At 25.80 the bristles make soft contact with the cheek. Begin a gentle upward sweep.
End at 26.00 with the bristles still touching the cheek, the sweep incomplete. Do not withdraw the brush, finish the stroke, fade out or freeze-frame.

AUDIO AND DIALOGUE
Exactly these spoken sounds and words:
Ghost at 0.00: "Boo!"
Witch near 1.10: "Mm-mm."
Witch near 2.10: "Mm-mm."
Witch near 12.00: "Mm-mm."
No other speech.
The ghost's scare is theatrical and brief. The witch's delivery is dry, low and unimpressed.
Detailed synchronized foley: lipstick cap, light chair movement, cosmetic taps, soft brush strokes, tweezers clicking, clothing rustle and one short hairspray hiss.
No generated background music. Music will be added continuously across both parts in editing.
No subtitles or written text.

CONTINUITY AND CONSTRAINTS
Makeover stages accumulate and never reset. Finished eyeshadow, liner, lashes, lipstick, updo, hair ornaments and choker remain present.
The ghost stays seated from 4.00 through the end.
The witch keeps the same outfit, hair and hat throughout.
No extra characters, anatomical distortions, detached hands, duplicate tools, floating accessories or tools passing through faces.
No belt, black wrap or sunglasses on the ghost yet.
The final brush contact is the precise handoff to Part 2.

Part 2
FORMAT
A 26.4-second VERTICAL 9:16 continuation of a premium stylized 3D animated gothic makeover comedy. This is Part 2 of a continuous 52.4-second film.
Match Part 1 in character design, rendering, light direction, color, room layout and animation timing. Natural-speed actions, no slow motion.

REFERENCES
@image1 defines the two principal characters:
LEFT = the witch makeup artist.
RIGHT = the original identity, proportions and base costume of the ghost client.
The ghost has already received her makeup and hairstyle in Part 1. Do NOT reset her to the unstyled reference appearance.

If supplied, @image2 is the final frame of Part 1 and defines the exact starting state, positions, lighting, hands and brush contact. Continue its action immediately.
Begin with a tighter view of that same moment, not a replay of the previous shot.
No video reference is required.

CHARACTER AND STARTING STATE
WITCH: shorter curvy adult woman, warm peach skin, amber eyes, dark purple waved hair with silver forelock, tilted black witch hat with plum band and gold crescent, gold crescent earrings, plum velvet midi dress, black belt and black heeled ankle boots.
Her design remains identical to @image1.

FIRST GHOST CLIENT: tall slender adult woman, pale gray-blue skin, large gray-green eyes and the same face as the right character in @image1.
At the beginning she already has smoky plum eyeshadow, dark eyeliner, long false lashes, berry-red lipstick, a tall secured dark updo with gold crescent and star ornaments, two loose face-framing strands, and a black choker with a gold crescent pendant.
She still wears her original ivory lace-collared dress and gray lace-up shoes. No belt, wrap or sunglasses yet.
She is seated, eyes closed, face turned slightly toward screen right.

SECOND GHOST CLIENT: a separate adult woman introduced only near the end. Pale gray skin, short messy chin-length black bob, round face, large dark eyes with tired shadows, no makeup and no jewelry. Worn light-gray ankle-length dress with ragged sleeves, dark flat shoes.
She carries a small rectangular quilted pink cosmetic bag with a zipper and top handle.
Make her visibly different from the first ghost. She is not a younger version, duplicate or reset of the first client.

ENVIRONMENT
The same gothic dressing room: dark plum walls, gold vanity and large bulb-lit mirror, candles, upper-corner cobwebs, two plum upholstered seats, cool blue rear doorway on screen right.
Witch stands on screen left of the seated first client.
A narrow black belt, black feather-textured wrap and jeweled cat-eye sunglasses wait on the vanity. The witch's smartphone lies beside the cosmetics.
An open doorway and clear walkway remain behind the clients.
Keep reflections, props and furniture consistent.

TIMELINE
All timestamps below are local to Part 2.

0.00-2.00 | CONTINUE THE POWDER STROKE
Start immediately on a tight close-up of the first ghost's RIGHT cheek, visible on screen left.
The witch's RIGHT hand holds the fluffy brush, whose bristles are already touching the cheek at the exact contact position established at the end of Part 1.
Continue the unfinished gentle upward sweep and lift the brush away naturally. Do not approach the cheek again or repeat the stroke.
The ghost opens her eyes.
Cut wider as the witch lowers the brush and places it and the compact on the vanity.

2.00-4.40 | WAIT
The first ghost leans forward, places both feet beneath her and stands from the client chair, pleased with the result.
The witch notices and raises an open palm to stop her.
At approximately 3.00, the witch says, "Wait."
The ghost stops beside the chair and looks down toward the witch, curious rather than frightened.

4.40-6.50 | THE OUTFIT
The witch briefly looks over the ghost's plain ivory dress, then gestures toward the accessories on the vanity.
At approximately 5.00 she says, "The outfit."
The ghost glances down at her dress, then back toward the accessories and gives a small understanding nod.
Keep her standing for the remaining styling steps. Do not sit her down again.
Do not remove, replace or magically repair her original dress.

6.50-8.70 | BELT
Cut to the waist. The witch wraps the prepared narrow black belt around the ivory dress, feeds it through its round gold buckle and fastens it.
The fabric gathers naturally beneath the belt. The witch straightens the buckle once and releases it.
The belt is now permanently part of the completed outfit.

8.70-10.70 | BLACK WRAP
Medium shot. The witch takes the black feather-textured wrap from the vanity and places it around the ghost's shoulders.
Its two ends settle down the front and sides of the dress.
The ghost catches the ends lightly with her hands as the witch releases them.
Show soft weight, trailing fibers and natural fabric overlap.

10.70-12.20 | SUNGLASSES
Close-up of the ghost. The witch opens a pair of dark cat-eye sunglasses with a sparkling pale jeweled frame and slides the arms over the ears.
The glasses rest correctly on the bridge of the nose.
The witch withdraws both hands. Hair ornaments, choker, makeup and hairstyle remain unchanged.

12.20-14.00 | COMPLETE REVEAL
Full-body view. The first ghost straightens her posture, shifts her weight onto one leg and lifts her chin with newfound confidence.
One hand rests near her waist, the other lightly holds the wrap.
The witch smiles proudly beside her.
The transformation is entirely makeup, hair, accessories and attitude. Preserve the ghost's original body and face.

14.00-18.50 | SELFIE
The witch picks up her smartphone from the vanity and holds it in her LEFT hand, extended slightly upward and toward screen left.
The first ghost leans closer without removing her glasses. Both look toward the phone's front camera lens.
Around 15.00, the witch playfully says, "Loopa-sa-loopa."
Treat this as a short cheerful nonsense phrase, not a magical spell. It causes no visual transformation.
Cut to a close two-shot of their faces from approximately the phone's viewpoint, then back to a wider view revealing the phone in the witch's hand.
They hold one clear pose. A single camera shutter click marks one photograph.
No readable phone interface or on-screen text.

18.50-20.50 | CLIENT LEAVES
The witch lowers the phone and sets it on the vanity.
The finished first client turns and walks across the foreground toward screen right, the black wrap swaying behind her.
As she moves out, reveal the second ghost client standing in the blue-lit rear doorway with the pink cosmetic bag held in front of her.
Briefly show both ghosts in the same frame: the finished first client exiting in the foreground and the unstyled second client in the doorway.
Then the first client exits completely and does not return.

20.50-22.50 | ME NEXT?
The second client steps through the doorway, pauses beside the now-vacant client chair and raises the pink cosmetic bag to chest height with both hands.
At approximately 21.00, she asks hopefully, "Me next?"
The witch turns toward her.
The bag remains a cosmetic bag, not a phone or handbag of changing shape.

22.50-24.70 | THE PUNCHLINE
Close-up of the witch. She gives a tired sideways look, slightly lowers her eyelids and exhales.
Beginning around 23.00, she says clearly, "You have got to be kidding me."
Deliver the line with weary disbelief, not shouting or aggression.
Keep the entire sentence intelligible before the final action.

24.70-26.40 | ANOTHER MAKEOVER BEGINS
Cut to the established medium two-shot.
The second client sits on the same client chair and places the pink cosmetic bag securely on her lap.
At the same time, the witch opens a hinged cosmetic case on the vanity and takes out a large clean powder brush.
The witch turns toward the new client's face and raises the brush.
End as the bristles make the first gentle contact with the new client's cheek.
No full transformation of the second client. End on the implication that the process is starting again.

AUDIO AND DIALOGUE
Use exactly these English lines:
Witch at local 3.00: "Wait."
Witch at local 5.00: "The outfit."
Witch at local 15.00: "Loopa-sa-loopa."
Second ghost at local 21.00: "Me next?"
Witch beginning at local 23.00: "You have got to be kidding me."

The witch sounds confident and dry, briefly playful for the selfie, then comically weary at the end.
The second ghost sounds shy and hopeful.
Continuous quiet room ambience matching Part 1.
Synchronized sounds: the continuing soft brush sweep, compact placed on wood, subtle chair movement, belt buckle click, wrap rustle, glasses handling, one selfie shutter click, footsteps, cosmetic case latch and brushes shifting.
No generated background music. Add one continuous music track across both parts during editing.
No additional dialogue, narration, subtitles or end titles.

CONTINUITY AND EDITING
The opening is a cut on the same powder-brush action, with tighter framing. No repeated approach, repeated stroke or reset of the makeover.
The completed first client's makeup, updo, ornaments and choker are present from the first frame. Belt, wrap and sunglasses appear only when physically applied, then remain until she exits.
Her original ivory dress and gray shoes remain throughout.
The second ghost appears only after the first client's selfie. She keeps her short bob, unstyled face and pink cosmetic bag.
Exactly three distinct characters appear across this part: one witch and two separate ghost clients.
No character duplication, face swapping, disappearing props, broken reflections, floating tools or anatomical distortions.
No fade-in, fade-out, recap, introductory shot or pause at the beginning.
```
