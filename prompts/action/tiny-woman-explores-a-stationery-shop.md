```text
STYLE: Photorealistic live-action miniature-person FX, cinematic macro photography, ultra-shallow depth of field, authentic South Asian stationary shop, dynamic handheld camera, dramatic practical lighting, realistic physics, detailed paper textures, premium viral commercial aesthetic, 4K.

Create a 30-second cinematic adventure featuring one athletic miniature woman at approximately 1 5 scale inside a busy stationary shop. Maintain her exact face, hairstyle, outfit, body proportions, backpack, and miniature scale consistently throughout the entire video.

She begins by running across a towering stack of notebooks, leaping from one notebook to another as if crossing rooftops. She slides down the sloping cover of a giant register and jumps onto a massive pile of colorful pens. She balances on a giant pencil as it rolls across the counter, jumping over scattered erasers and sharpeners. A huge stack of papers suddenly collapses, sending sheets flying everywhere; she grabs the corner of one sheet and rides it like a parachute before landing on an oversized geometry box.

Next, she climbs a giant ruler, races across its measurement markings, and jumps onto a rotating globe. She holds on as the globe spins, then leaps onto a hanging display of colorful markers. She swings between oversized scissors and rulers, narrowly avoiding a giant stapler pressing down nearby. She then slides through a tunnel formed by stacked notebooks, dodges rolling glue sticks, and jumps over a massive calculator whose buttons look like platforms.

For the final sequence, she climbs a towering display of sketchbooks and reaches the top shelf. She stands proudly beside a giant pencil while the camera pulls back, revealing the entire stationary shop, its endless shelves of supplies, and customers moving in the background. The final wide shot emphasizes how tiny she is in this enormous world.

Use dynamic macro tracking shots, dramatic low angles, smooth aerial-style camera movements, strong foreground parallax, realistic reflections and shadows, shallow depth of field, detailed paper fibers, glossy pen surfaces, natural human movement, believable physics, seamless transitions, and cinematic lighting. Keep the character recognizable and her scale consistent in every shot.

End with the cinematic title: “SMALL SCALE. BIG ADVENTURE.” followed by “EVERY DESK HAS A STORY.”

SFX: Tiny footsteps, paper rustling, pencil rolling, notebook covers creaking, pen clattering, paper fluttering, stapler clunks, calculator button clicks, shop ambience, rushing air, and cinematic impact sounds.

NEGATIVE: No cartoon, anime, toy-like appearance, animation, duplicated character, face or outfit changes, distorted anatomy, floating, inconsistent scale, unrealistic physics, narration, or subtitles.
```
