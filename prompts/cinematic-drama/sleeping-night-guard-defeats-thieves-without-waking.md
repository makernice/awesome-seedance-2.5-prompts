```text
30s comedic warehouse scene. Character matches the reference image exactly in face/outfit. Night watchwoman asleep in a chair at a warehouse entrance, feet on a crate, dim lighting, peaceful snoring. A gang of five thieves sneaks in, mocking her.
0–3s: Thieves try to shove past her. Without waking, her arm clotheslines the first thief; he crashes down.
3–6s: Another thief approaches; her sleepy foot trips him into crates. A third gets his ankle caught by her lazy hand and falls.
6–9s: Remaining thieves huddle nervously. The leader charges her; she casually swings an elbow into his stomach while still asleep. He collapses.
9–13s: Wide shot: four thieves sprawled around her chair. The last thief cautiously retreats, but her stretching leg catches his ankle, sending him face-first into boxes.
13–16s: All five thieves lie defeated around her. Close-up: she remains peacefully asleep, smiling and snoring. One thief crawls away; her foot casually rests on his back like a footrest.
16–20s: Silent comedic aftermath. Thieves groan while she keeps sleeping. Distant police sirens and flashing lights approach.
20–24s: One eye slowly opens. She scans the defeated thieves with an unimpressed deadpan look, then gives a tiny shrug and closes it again. Snoring resumes.
24–27s: Police rush in, stop in disbelief at the scene, and cautiously approach her. One officer says, “Uh, you’re gonna want to see this.”
27–30s: Officer gently wakes her; she lazily waves him off without opening her eyes. Final wide shot: police handle the handcuffed thieves while she sleeps peacefully through everything. Cinematic comedic ending.
```
