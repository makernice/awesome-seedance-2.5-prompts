Cinematic emotional fantasy short film, photorealistic, 16:9, 24 seconds, one continuous story with smooth cinematic cuts. No text, no subtitles, no dialogue, no weapons, no violence.

CHARACTERS (keep identical in every shot):
- The woman: early 20s, pale skin, calm dark eyes, very long straight black hair soaked by rain and sticking to her cheeks and shoulders, wearing a floor-length dark charcoal hooded cloak of heavy wool over a simple black dress, the hood down. Same face, hair and cloak throughout.
- The dragon: huge but gentle, matte charcoal-black scales with subtle blue sheen, long curved horns, a soft rounded snout, large glowing warm amber eyes, folded leathery wings beaded with rain. Slow, careful, curious movements, never aggressive.

SHOTS:
0-3s: Extreme wide shot from behind and slightly above her, she stands still on the cliff edge, cloak whipping in the wind, heavy rain, the dragon's silhouette slowly emerges from the fog ahead, amber eyes glowing.
3-6s: Medium low-angle shot, the dragon steps closer and slowly lowers its huge head to her level, rain streaming off its horns, a soft breath of mist from its nostrils.
6-9s: Close-up of her face, rain running down her skin and lashes, wet hair on her cheeks, calm and emotional, eyes glistening, a slow deep breath.
9-12s: Medium shot, she slowly raises her open right hand toward the dragon, palm forward, trembling slightly; the dragon stays still and blinks.
12-15s: Extreme close-up, her fingertips gently touch the wet scales of the dragon's snout, raindrops bounce off the scales, the dragon's amber eye softens and half-closes.
15-18s: Wide shot, the clouds part above them, the first golden sunbeams sweep across the cliff, the rain thins into a few last glowing drops.
18-21s: Close two-shot in profile, she rests her forehead against the dragon's snout, eyes closed, peaceful; the dragon gently exhales, mist swirling in the golden light.
21-24s: Final slow push-in close-up of her peaceful face in soft warm golden light, a faint smile, wet hair drying in the breeze; hold until the end.
