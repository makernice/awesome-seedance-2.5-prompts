```text
GETTING READY FOR HER FIRST JOB INTERVIEW

Create an ultra-realistic personal vlog filmed by a friend using a consumer MiniDV camcorder in South Korea in 2003. A young Korean woman nervously prepares for her first job interview in her modest bedroom, from choosing her outfit to rushing out the door.

CHARACTER & CONTINUITY

A Korean woman in her early 20s with a soft oval face, natural skin texture, shoulder-length dark brown hair, and side-swept bangs. She starts in a T-shirt and home trousers, then changes into a white blouse, dark knee-length skirt, dark cardigan, and black shoes. Maintain consistent identity, hairstyle, outfit, and props.

SCENE 1 — OUTFIT (0–5s)

She compares two cardigans against her interview clothes, hesitates, chooses the darker one, and gives the camera a nervous smile. A wooden desk, paper calendar, and handwritten interview note establish the year.

SCENE 2 — IRONING (5–10s)

She carefully irons her blouse, struggles briefly with a sleeve, smooths it out, and checks the collar. A corded telephone and household calendar appear in the background. The iron stays safely on the board when not in use.

SCENE 3 — PRACTICE (10–15s)

Dressed for the interview, she practices her introduction in a mirror, forgets her words, checks handwritten notes, and tries again. She notices the camera and laughs shyly.

SCENE 4 — DOCUMENTS (15–20s)

She organizes her résumé and application papers into a folder. One page falls; she retrieves it and puts it back. Seeing the clock, she places the folder in her shoulder bag and checks for her purse and keys.

SCENE 5 — FINAL CHECK (20–26s)

She smooths her blouse, adjusts her cardigan, fixes her collar, and tucks back loose hair. After attempting a confident smile, she notices the time and grabs her bag in a hurry.

SCENE 6 — DEPARTURE (26–30s)

She hurries to the door, checks her keys, slips into her black shoes, and gives the camera one last nervous smile. The camera follows her into the hallway before an abrupt recording cut.

VISUAL STYLE & SOUND

Authentic early-2000s MiniDV footage: soft SD detail, mild video noise, imperfect handheld framing, autofocus hunting, accidental zooms, and brief exposure shifts. Use natural lighting, realistic colors, and period-appropriate Korean apartment interiors. Include fabric rustling, ironing, paper shuffling, faint clock ticking, footsteps, jingling keys, and the door opening. Dialogue is quiet, natural Korean speech.

CONTINUITY RULES

Keep facial identity, anatomy, lighting, and room layout consistent. Use a natural transition for the outfit change. Once dressed, her outfit stays unchanged. Documents remain in the folder and bag after packing; account for the keys before departure.

Avoid modern technology, cinematic grading, fake VHS overlays, exaggerated acting, face morphing, duplicated fingers, teleportation, instant outfit changes, and background music.

Mood: A candid, slightly clumsy, believable memory of a nervous young Korean woman preparing for her first job interview in 2003.
```
